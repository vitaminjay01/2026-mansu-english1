4Grammar · 비교급과 최상급

두 개를 비교할 땐 비교급 + than, 셋 이상 중에서 '가장 ~한'은 the + 최상급!

🟠 비교급 (A가 B보다 더 ~하다)
Mauna Kea is taller than Mount Everest.
Antarctica is larger than the Sahara.
A watch is more expensive than a pen.
🟣 최상급 (가장 ~하다)
Antarctica is the largest desert in the world.
Angel Falls is the tallest waterfall in the world.
This is the most difficult question of all.
